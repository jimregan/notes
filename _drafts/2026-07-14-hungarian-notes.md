## 22. De azt azért mondhatom, nem?

```text
De   az-t      azért     mond-hat-om,       nem?
but  that-ACC  for.that  say-POT-1SG.DEF    NEG
```

**Literal translation:** But I can say that, for that reason, can I not?

**Contextual reading:** But surely I am allowed to say this, aren’t I?

**Notes:**
Here **azért** is discourse-pragmatic rather than expressing a clear literal cause. It gives the sense “at least,” “though,” or “surely.”

The actual content of **azt**, “that,” follows in the next lines.

## 28. De valahol csak van tere ennek az absztrakt gondolatnak is…

```text
De   vala-hol   csak    van       ter-e
but  some-where surely  be.3SG    space-3SG.POSS

ennek     az   absztrakt  gondolat-nak  is.
this.DAT  the  abstract   thought-DAT   also
```

**Literal translation:** But somewhere there surely is space of this abstract thought too.

**Less awkward but structurally close:** But surely there is room somewhere for this abstract thought too.

**Notes:**
This is a Hungarian possessive construction:

```text
ennek a gondolatnak a tere
this.DAT the thought.DAT the space.POSS
the space of this thought
```

The article before **tere** is absent because the phrase occurs after existential **van**.

## 29. …hogy azt mondhassam, hogy a vonat szabjon rád rövidnadrágot.

```text
hogy  az-t      mond-hat-j-am,
that  that-ACC  say-POT-SBJV-1SG.DEF

hogy  a    vonat  szab-j-on
that  the  train  tailor-SBJV-3SG.INDF

rád       rövid-nadrág-ot.
onto.2SG  short-trousers-ACC
```

**Literal translation:** …that I may be able to say that the train should tailor shorts onto you.

**Notes:**
The surface form **mondhassam** contains assimilation:

```text
mond-hat-j-am → mondhassam
say-POT-SBJV-1SG.DEF
```

**Szabni valakire valamit** can mean to cut or tailor a garment to fit someone. The line imagines the train “tailoring shorts” onto the person. The implication that the train removes the person’s lower legs is pragmatic rather than overtly stated.

## 31. Meg hogy a hullazsák cipzárja csípné be a mellszőrödet.

```text

and  that  the  corpse-bag  zipper-3SG.POSS

csíp-né             be  a    mell-szőr-öd-et.
pinch-COND.3SG.DEF  PV  the  chest-hair-2SG.POSS-ACC
```

**Literal translation:** And that the body bag’s zipper would pinch in your chest hair.

**Normal English description:** And that the zipper of a body bag would catch your chest hair.

**Notes:**
The prefix **be** follows the conditional verb: **csípné be**. The compound verb **becsíp** means to trap or catch something by closing around it.

## 32. Te féregarcú pudvás fostalicska, tyúkbaszó, gedvás kis köcsög.

```text
Te   féreg-arc-ú    pudvás  fos-talicska,
2SG  worm-face-PROP rotten  shit-wheelbarrow

tyúk-basz-ó,  gedvás  kis     köcsög.
hen-fuck-AGT filthy   little  jug
```

**Strict literal translation:** You worm-faced rotten shit-wheelbarrow, hen-fucker, filthy little jug.

**Abusive meaning:** You worm-faced, rotten shit-cart, chicken-fucking, filthy little asshole.

**Notes:**
**Köcsög** literally means a type of jug or earthenware vessel. As an insult it means approximately “asshole/prick” and may carry a homophobic meaning.

**Pudvás** describes something rotten, decayed, porous, or mouldering.

**Gedvás** is a vulgar adjective meaning filthy, scabby, or disgusting.

# After the quotation

## 33. Ez egy pudekli volt, jó van?

```text
Ez    egy  pudekli  volt,        jó    van?
this  a    pudekli  be.PST.3SG   good  be.3SG
```

**Literal translation:** This was a pudekli, all right?

**Transcription note:**
I cannot confidently identify **pudekli**. It may be:

* a misheard word;
* a name or nonce formation;
* a reference requiring the audio or video context.

It is therefore left unsegmented and untranslated.

**Jó van** is a reduced colloquial pronunciation of standard **jól van**, “all right/okay.”

## 34. Jaj, hugyos, kapcsold le magad, mielőtt más is lekapcsol.

```text
Jaj,   hugy-os,     kapcsol-d          le  mag-ad,
INTJ   urine-ADJ    switch-IMP.2SG.DEF PV  self-2SG

mielőtt  más           is    le-kapcsol.
before   someone.else  also  PV-switch.3SG.INDF
```

**Literal translation:** Oh, urine-covered one, switch yourself off before someone else also switches [you] off.

**Notes:**
**Hugyos** literally means “urine-soaked,” “pissy,” or “one who pisses oneself.” As a vocative insult it may imply that someone is pathetic, childish, or contemptible.

The verb **lekapcsol** creates wordplay:

* literally: switch off;
* colloquially: catch, apprehend, stop, or take someone out.

**Magad** functions as the object “yourself.” A fuller accusative form **magadat** is also possible.

## 35. Ha nem ismerném jobban a Facebook-kommentelők jó szándékát, azt gondolnám, hogy ez egy fenyegetés.

```text

```

**Literal translation:** If I did not know the good intention of Facebook commenters better, I would think that this is a threat.

**Notes:**
The statement about their **jó szándék**, “good intention,” is sarcastic.

English “if I didn’t know better” is similar, but Hungarian overtly includes the object: **a Facebook-kommentelők jó szándékát**.

## 36. Egyébként meg ki kapcsolna le?

```text

```

**Literal translation:** And otherwise, who would switch [me] off?

**Natural contextual meaning:** Besides, who would take me out?

**Notes:**
The omitted object is understood as the speaker.

Here **meg** is the colloquial conjunction “and/as for that,” while **le** is the verbal prefix belonging to **lekapcsol**.

## 38. Asszem, törölt innen egy hozzászólást a némber.

## 40. A némber azt a hozzászólást törli, amelyiket csak kurvára akarja…

```text
A    némber  az-t      a    hozzá-szólás-t
the  broad   that-ACC  the  to.it-speak-NMLZ-ACC

törl-i,          amely-ik-et      csak
delete-3SG.DEF   which-ONE-ACC    simply

kurvá-ra    akar-ja.
whore-SBL   want-3SG.DEF
```

**Literal translation:** The broad deletes that comment which she simply to-a-whore wants.

That last phrase is morphologically literal but not semantically useful. Its actual sentence-level translation is:

**Close translation:** The broad deletes whichever comment she fucking wants [to delete].

**Notes:**
**Kurvára** is historically and morphologically **kurva + -ra**, literally something like “onto/to a whore.” Synchronically it is a grammaticalized vulgar intensifier meaning “fucking,” “damned,” or “extremely.”

The repeated verb “delete” is omitted after **akarja**, but understood:

```text
amelyiket csak kurvára akarja [törölni]
which.ACC she fucking wants [to delete]
```

**Amelyiket** contains **-ik**, which selects an individual member: “which one.”

## 41. …mert ez az ő oldala, vágod.

```text
mert     ez    az   ő    oldal-a,          vág-od.
because  this  the  3SG  page-3SG.POSS     cut-2SG.DEF
```

**Strict literal translation:** Because this is her page; you cut [it].

**Actual colloquial meaning:** Because this is her page, you get it.

**Notes:**
**Vágod?** is slang. The verb **vág** literally means “cut,” but **vágod?** commonly means “do you understand?” or “you get it?”
